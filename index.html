Gamepad: left stick pitch/roll, right stick or bumpers yaw, triggers throttle, A brake, Y reset, Back camera, Start hangar.

Tip: full throttle (Z), roll down the runway, pull back gently (S) around 60 kt. Rotorcraft: throttle ≈ 50% hovers. Blimp: power + pitch up to climb.
