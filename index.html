Gamepad: left stick pitch/roll, right stick or bumpers yaw, triggers throttle, A brake, Y new plane, LB+Y reset world, Back camera, Start hangar.

Tip: full throttle (Z), roll down the runway, pull back gently (S) around 60 kt. Rotorcraft: throttle ≈ 50% hovers. Blimp: power + pitch up to climb.

Studio: describe a craft, a model designs it, the sim flight-tests it, you fly it, then Refine with what felt wrong.
